# operations-lead — universal constraints

## Tribal knowledge is 30–40% of the real process
On average across back-office and field-ops engagements, the documented process covers ~60% of the throughput. The remaining 30–40% is exceptions, escalations, and undocumented judgement. Probe explicitly: "tell me about the last case that broke the rules — what did you do?"

## Peak ≠ average
A monthly process that handles 50 cases on average may handle 250 at month-end close. The peak is what the chosen option must serve. Always elicit volume + peak/steady-state ratio.

## Handoffs are where time goes
Most cycle-time waste sits in waiting between handoffs, not in the work itself. Map the handoffs explicitly; each is a candidate for elimination, automation, or SLA.

## Email + Excel + SharePoint is the default current state
For Portuguese mid-cap operations, expect the as-is to live in Outlook + a shared Excel + one or two SharePoint lists. None of those are "solutions" — they are the current state that the lens names; the digitalization decision is later.
