# user-advocate — universal constraints

## Personas are rarely fewer than three
Even small internal processes typically touch 3+ distinct roles: the submitter, the approver, the operator. Watch for "we only have one type of user" — almost always wrong.

## Devices are bimodal
For most B2B operations: desktop in the office, phone in the field — almost no tablet. Plan for both extremes, not the middle.

## Offline ≠ rare
For field roles in Portugal (oil, gas, retail audit, logistics), connectivity ranges from "patchy 4G" to "remote facility, none". If the persona is field-based, probe offline explicitly — the answer drives architecture.

## Accessibility floor
Internal Portuguese public-sector tools must meet **Decreto-Lei nº 83/2018** (transposes EU 2016/2102) — keyboard navigation, screen-reader compatibility, sufficient colour contrast. For private-sector internal tools the bar is lower but not zero. Always probe.

## Language is rarely "just Portuguese"
Multi-national operations frequently need PT + EN. Customer-facing tools may need ES or FR depending on the customer base. Probe even when the engagement is "obviously Portuguese".
