# Power Platform Research

This directory contains the research corpus used to author the aisa Power Platform (`pp`) pack.

## Important

This is RESEARCH.

It is NOT the final aisa pack.

Do not modify:

`library/packs/pp/`

during the research phase.

## Objective

Build an evidence-backed knowledge base that will later be transformed into:

`library/packs/pp/`

## Principle

Research first.

Structure knowledge second.

Author the pack third.

Validate the pack last.

## Core rule

Power Platform must NOT be assumed to be the answer.

The research must identify:

* when Power Platform is appropriate;
* when it is conditionally appropriate;
* when it is inappropriate;
* what requirements influence that decision;
* what architectural patterns apply;
* what risks and constraints exist;
* what alternatives should be considered.
